Question Selection Rules

  1. Match the candidate's career path and field.
  2. Filter by target role or government service.
  3. Filter by interview purpose and interview type.
  4. Filter by difficulty: Guided, Standard, or Challenging.
  5. Prioritize the listed field topics, distributing questions across topics when possible.
  6. Return the requested question count, from 1 to 20.
Example: Government → Banking → RBI Grade B Officer → Exam preparation → Standard should return questions tagged Banking, RBI Grade B Officer, Exam preparation, Standard, and topics such as banking awareness, quantitative aptitude, reasoning ability, and financial awareness.
